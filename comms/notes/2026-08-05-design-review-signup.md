# 2026-08-05 — Design review: the signed-out funnel

Raw notes. Marguerite, Wren, Tobias, plus Priyanka from ops for the last ten minutes.

---

Started on the hero. Marguerite walked the desktop and the 320 version. Nobody argued with
the layout, which is usually a sign we're looking at the wrong thing.

Wren asked what happens when a cohort is full. Long pause. Nobody knew. Right now the card
shows the start date and the session count and that's it — a learner clicks through, gets to
the course page, and finds out there. Priyanka confirmed ops gets those emails and there are
more of them than anyone thinks: **LT-0912 filled in nine days and we took eleven enquiries
after it closed.**

So **sold out is a state we don't have a design for**, and it isn't just the card. It's the
catalog, the course page, and whatever we say instead of "enrol."

Tobias raised the related one — what a signed-out learner sees for a cohort that has already
started. Same gap. Different answer, probably.

Marguerite's point, and it landed: we keep designing the happy path at two widths and calling
it done. Nobody has written down what this flow actually owes. **We don't have a list.**

Wren: pricing. Anonymous is the only state that sees it, which makes it the only state where
getting it wrong is a public problem rather than an internal one.

Argued about whether waitlist is a product decision or a design one. Didn't settle it.
Marguerite is going to lay out what states exist before anyone designs anything else, on the
grounds that we can't have the waitlist argument until we can see the grid.
