# Comms

Meeting notes, raw transcripts, status reports. **The unprocessed layer.**

Everything here is a record of something that was said, not a decision about what is true.
That distinction is the whole point of the folder.

- Nothing in here is canonical. If a meeting settled something, the decision goes in
  `../product/decisions/` and this folder keeps the transcript that led to it.
- Raw is fine. Do not clean these up. A summarized transcript has already thrown away the
  half-sentence that turns out to matter three weeks later.
- Date-prefix every file: `2026-08-04-<what-it-was>.md`.

## Why it isn't in `deliverables/`

A project folder holds what a project needs. Comms cuts across projects — one status
report covers three of them, and one leadership review changes two roadmaps at once.
Filing it under a project means the other two teams never find it.

## Why an agent should read it carefully

This is the folder most likely to make a model confidently wrong. Someone proposing
something in a meeting reads exactly like someone deciding it. When you cite anything from
here, say it came from a meeting and name the date — and check `../product/decisions/`
before you report it as settled.
