# 2026-08-12 — Pre-start schedule edits need one approval, not two

status: decided

A schedule edit before a cohort starts goes out with one approval, not two. The
two-approval bar in `../../team/how-we-work.md` is for anything touching enrollment or
money, and a pre-start schedule edit touches neither — no learner has enrolled against
the old time yet, so there's nothing to reconcile.

Raised because SCRUM-6 was sitting in review waiting on a second approver who didn't have
context on the ops-error audit this project is built on. One approver who's read
`../../projects/cohort-scheduling/deliverables/research/ops-error-audit.md` is a better
bar than two who haven't.

Does not touch the after-start question — that's still unscoped, per
`../../team/roadmap-and-bets.md`.
