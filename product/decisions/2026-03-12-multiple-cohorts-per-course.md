# 2026-03-12 — A course can have many cohorts

status: decided — reverses 2025-11-03

Instructors were duplicating courses to re-run them, which broke every link anyone had
shared and made the catalog unreadable. Nineteen support tickets in Q1, all the same shape.

Instructor Tools took the content-model versioning work after all. What changed their mind
was the duplicate-course count, not the argument.

**Why the old entry is still in this folder:** "we decided X, then reversed it four months
later" is the useful thing. Editing the November entry to say the opposite would have
destroyed the only part worth keeping.
