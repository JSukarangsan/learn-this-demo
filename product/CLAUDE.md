# Product

Read on top of `../CLAUDE.md`. Only what's different for product work goes here.

- `decisions/` — why we landed where we did. Newest last. **Never edit an old entry** —
  a correction is a new entry. The value is in the sequence.
- `glossary.md` — product terms. Metric definitions live in `../insights/`, not here.
- `procedures/` — things you run, not things you read.

## Every decision entry carries a status

`decided` · `proposed` · `ruled out by another team`

Without it, an entry reads as settled, and the next person to ask gets told something
is happening that isn't. A record without its status doesn't fail to help — it produces
confident error.
