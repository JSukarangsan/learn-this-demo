# Who owns what

Lives in `team/` because it changes about as often as the charter does — and like the
charter, it is true regardless of what we are building this quarter.

| What | Owns it | Where to ask | Who actually unblocks a review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enrollment flow | Webapp | `#webapp` | us |
| Payments and seats | Billing | `#billing` — they triage Tuesdays | Wren |
| Live session video | the vendor | vendor support, 24h SLA | escalate via Tobias |
| Course content model | Instructor Tools | weekly sync, not Slack | Marguerite |
| Lifecycle email | Growth | `#growth` | whoever is on rotation |

Two columns people skip, and why they're here:

- **Where to ask.** Billing triages on Tuesdays. A Thursday message sits for four days,
  and you should know that before you're blocked rather than after.
- **Who actually unblocks.** The team that owns something and the person who will move
  your request are frequently not the same. `CODEOWNERS` says one thing; this says what's true.

**This table is only useful if it's complete.** Eighty percent coverage is close to
worthless — the missing rows are exactly where people get stuck, and now they've stopped
asking around because a document exists. If you hit something not listed, add the row.
