# Team — Webapp

Read on top of `../CLAUDE.md`. This is the stable layer: who we are, what we're trying to
do this year, who decides things, and how we run. **It changes rarely** — quarterly is a
normal refresh cadence, and if you're editing it monthly something is wrong.

Everything here is the context every project inherits without asking for it.

- `charter.md` — what this team is for, and what it isn't
- `goals-and-okrs.md` — the targets for the year, and the guardrails
- `roadmap-and-bets.md` — what we're actually doing, and what we said no to
- `key-stakeholders.md` — who decides, who to loop in, and what each one pushes on
- `how-we-work.md` — cadence, rituals, review bar, channels
- `ownership.md` — the routing table: who owns what, where to ask, who actually unblocks

One copy of each thing. If you find yourself restating a metric definition here to make a
goal readable, link it instead — two copies drift and then nobody knows which is right.
