# Instructor interviews — 2026-07-14

Three instructors, 30 min each. Raw, not cleaned up. **This is deliberate** — it's more
useful to read what they said than a summary of what they said.

---

**R. (runs 6 cohorts/yr, mostly corporate)**

"I send the dates in an email and then I wait. Sometimes two days. I don't know if it's
in until a learner tells me."

Asked what she'd want: "just let me type them." Pushed on recurring — "no, honestly every
cohort is different, I'd end up fighting the recurrence."

On timezone: "the cohort has a timezone. It's whatever the client is. I'm not in it and
that's fine, I'm used to it." — this is the sentence the whole timezone model came from.

---

**M. (2 cohorts/yr, solo)**

Didn't care about scheduling. Spent most of the time on something else: he can't tell
which learners are org-assigned and which bought their own seat, and he treats those
groups differently in the first session. Logged separately; not this project.

"The email thing is fine. It's like once a year for me."

---

**J. (12+ cohorts/yr, agency)**

The strongest ask, and the reason the edit path is in scope: "I change the schedule maybe
half the time after I've sent it. Something moves. Right now that's another email and I'm
never sure which version is live."

Asked about editing after start: "that'd be nice but honestly no, once we've started I
wouldn't touch it. People have booked their time."

Confirms the out-of-scope call. Worth keeping — this is the one instructor who'd have
justified building it.
